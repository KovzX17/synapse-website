About Synapse

We connect healthcare needs through CashlessHub — Software, ClaimXpert — Cashless Assistance Service (RCM), Marketing — Digital, LegitMate — Legal Claim Expert, CareWell — Home Health Services, and Virtual Medical Assessment — Skilled Medical Professionals Enrolment for Underwriting.

Who We Are

We are a team of inspired, enthusiastic, motivated and dedicated healthcare professionals. Together we have an experience of 35 years in the Healthcare Industry.

We have seen the “Dashavatar” between the Payer–Provider, Provider–Patient, Patient–Payer, and the dilemma which each “Doctor” or “Professional” undergoes regarding his hospital business. Complying with cashless legalities is another herculean task.

We work to keep the Clinician inside you alive — and your business to optimize.

Why “Synapse”

We at Synapse strongly believe that there are numerous signals to be received and transmitted among the various stakeholders — the Client – Payer – Provider trio in the Healthcare Ecosystem.

Receiving and transmitting the correct signals at the correct time enhances service delivery to the client for his satisfaction and delight — resulting in the profitability of the payer and provider.
